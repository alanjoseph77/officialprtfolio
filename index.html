Digital Twin

Cyber-Physical Systems & Live Visualization

Cyber-Physical Lab Simulation

Three.js, Node.js, MQTT, ESP32, REST API, Digital Twin

• Real sensor events from an ESP32 (RFID, PIR, DHT22) publish over MQTT to a Node.js broker that bridges live door and robot state; the browser polls REST endpoints every 200ms so the Three.js 3D scene stays in sync.
• Six-axis robot arm kinematics, door open/close sequences, and furnace operations are all triggered by physical hardware events, giving a live visual mirror of the lab floor.
• Detailed 3D sensor models rendered via esp32-assembly.js and sensor-assembly.js; multiple camera views and a sidebar console display real-time MQTT logs.

Industrial Molding Station

Three.js, Digital Twin, AnimationMixer, Procedural Meshes

• A real-time digital twin of an industrial injection molding station every component mirroring its physical counterpart in code.
• Multi-part assembly featuring pistons, clamping plates, guide rods, and ejector systems with live mechanical animations.
• Enables remote monitoring, predictive maintenance simulation, and operator training without physical hardware.
